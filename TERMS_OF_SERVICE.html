4Moderation decisions are made by people

YORI surfaces information for staff and carries out actions that staff direct. Nearly every enforcement decision against an individual is made or approved by a human staff member, and YORI does not independently decide to ban or kick anyone. The one exception is an optional, off-by-default anti-spam mode that can apply a temporary Discord timeout on its own and, when enabled, remove the offending messages; staff are notified of every one and can lift a timeout, though removed messages are not restored. It is described in full in the Privacy Policy. Questions about a moderation decision, including appeals, should be directed to the community's staff.
