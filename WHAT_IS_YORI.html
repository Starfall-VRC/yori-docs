Moderation bot for VRChat and Discord

What is YORI?

An independent moderation bot that helps the staff of The Virtual Reality Show (TVRS) keep their VRChat community and Discord server safe.

A Discord and VRChat bot, built and run by Starfall. An independent project - not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord or VRChat.

In short

  • YORI is a tool for the volunteer staff who run a community - it gives them a shared, accountable record of moderation decisions across both VRChat and Discord.
  • It helps people moderate; it does not replace them. Staff make every decision.
  • It does not read the content of your messages, it never bans anyone on its own, and it does not sell data.

The short version

YORI is the moderation assistant for the TVRS VRChat group and its associated Discord server. Running a welcoming community takes a team of volunteers, often across different time zones, and keeping everyone on the same page is hard. YORI gives that team one shared, consistent place to record what happened and what was done about it, so decisions are fair, transparent to staff, and not lost when someone is offline.

What it helps staff do

  • Keep one shared history. When staff take a moderation action, YORI records what happened, which rule applied, and who handled it - so the team has a clear, consistent account rather than scattered notes.
  • See the picture across both platforms. Staff can look up what is already on record for a member on VRChat and on Discord in one place.
  • Get a heads-up on unusual activity. YORI can raise advisory alerts - for example, if a lot of new accounts arrive at once - so a human can take a look and decide whether anything needs doing.

People make the decisions

This is the part that matters most: YORI never bans, kicks, or mutes anyone on its own. Its automated checks only raise a flag for a person to review. Every enforcement decision is made or approved by a human staff member, and questions about a decision go to the community's staff. YORI is a tool that helps good moderators be consistent - not an automated judge.

Privacy and respect

YORI is built to collect as little as it needs to do its job. It does not read the content of your messages, it keeps its records private to the community's staff, and it never sells data or shares it with advertisers. You can ask staff to see, correct, or delete information held about you. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

What YORI is not

  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord or VRChat.
  • It is not a public service you sign up for - it is a tool used by one community's own staff.
  • It is not surveillance. It does not follow you around, read your conversations, or track you outside the moderation records described in the Privacy Policy.
